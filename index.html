UK Cubing League

In the even weeks of term we compete in the UK Cubing League, which is a competition between university societies accross the country.
Each meetup we do timed solves of four puzzles out of a rotating selecion of both WCA and non-WCA events.
You can find out more and see our progress on the UKCL Instagram.
